Clock In / Out Reminders

Emails and mobile notifications telling an employee they have not clocked in or out yet. Sent 15 minutes after a scheduled shift starts or ends.

Violations Alerts

A daily mobile notification (after 10:00) telling supervisors their team has unresolved violations, overtime or absences from the last 5 days. Only users with the Violations tile receive it.

Timesheet Exceptions

Shows employees their own absences, missing punches and variances from the last 7 days in the ESS inbox, with a mobile notification when a new one appears. Turning this off only hides the inbox items - the exceptions stay on the timesheet.

ESS Approvals

Shows managers pending leave requests, profile changes and attendance exceptions in the ESS inbox, with a mobile notification when a new one arrives. Approvals can still be done from the normal screens when this is off.
