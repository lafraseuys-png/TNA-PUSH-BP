Labour law reference (BCEA) for information only

Who gets overtime and how it is paid

  • Covered: Employees earning up to the earnings threshold (R269,600.90 a year from 1 May 2026)
  • Not covered: Senior managers, travelling sales staff who set their own hours, people working under 24 hours a month, and anyone above the threshold (their contract decides)
  • Overtime pay: At least 1.5 × the normal wage
  • Time off instead: Only if agreed: at least 90 min paid time off per overtime hour (or normal pay + 30 min off). Given within 1 month, up to 12 months by written agreement
  • Sunday work: 2 × the wage (1.5 × if they normally work Sundays)
  • Public holiday worked: At least 2 × the normal day's wage

Legal limits

  • Normal hours in a week: 45 hours
  • Normal hours in a day: 9 hours (5 days a week or fewer), 8 hours (more than 5 days)
  • Overtime in a day: 3 hours
  • Overtime in a week: 10 hours (up to 15 for 2 months a year by collective agreement)
  • Total in a day, with overtime: 12 hours
  • Overtime itself: Only by agreement with the employee

South Africa, Basic Conditions of Employment Act (sections 6, 9, 10, 16, 18). The earnings threshold changes most years - check the latest gazette. A bargaining council or sectoral determination can set different rules for your industry.

T&A Engine Configurations

Ignore overtime in Threshold

When someone clocks in a little early or out a little late (inside the shift's before/after limits), it is not counted as overtime. They are paid for the shift times only. A shift can have its own setting for this.

Make up missed normal time first

If someone comes in late or leaves early, any extra time they work first makes up the missing normal hours. Only what is left over counts as overtime. Example: 30 min late, then stays 1 hour after the shift = 30 min normal time + 30 min overtime.

Ignore double clockings

If someone clocks more than once on the same device within this many minutes, only the first clocking counts. This stops accidental double scans.

Minutes

Send Changed Photos to Phones

When an employee's photo is added or changed, the phones download the new photo on their next sync. Off: phones only get a photo for employees they have no photo for (the old way). "Send all photos" makes every phone download all employee photos again on its next sync.

Auto-Ignore Geofence Violations

If enabled, punches that are clocked outside of the employee's assigned geofence radius will be automatically ignored by the Nightly Analyzer. You can manually restore them in the Team View.

Display Deactivated Departments

If enabled, deactivated departments will remain visible in dropdowns and the department defaults grid.

Keep Filters When Switching Tabs

The last department, dates and employee search picked on one TNA tab are used on the next tab you open (Team View, Roster, Attendance, Time Logs, Violations, OT, Reports, Maps...). Tabs limited to 7 days use the first 7 days. Remembered per browser tab until it is closed.

How hours are shown

How hours appear on timesheets and Excel reports: 8 and a half hours as 8.50 or as 08:30.

Night shifts that cross midnight

Which day the hours belong to when a night shift runs into a weekend or public holiday. Example: Friday 22:00 to Saturday 06:00.

After hours are recalculated

When new clockings or roster changes recalculate a day, which Manager Inbox items go back to waiting for approval. "Force Overwrite All" always sends everything back.

Engine Options

Optional calculation rules. The defaults keep hours exactly as they were calculated before. A change applies to days calculated from now on; use the Bulk Analyzer (or the recalculate pop-up) for days already calculated. Approved and payroll-locked days are never changed.

Overtime Past a Threshold Counts From

When someone stays past the After Threshold (or arrives before the Before Threshold). Example: shift ends 17:00, threshold 30, out 17:45 → 45 min (shift end) or 15 min (past the threshold).

Round Normal Time to the Half Hour

On a day with overtime, Normal time of x:01–x:02 or x:28–x:29 snaps to the half hour and the minutes move to or from OT (in at 08:02 → Normal 8:00, OT 0:43). Off: Normal 7:58, OT 0:45.

"Force Sunday Rate" Shifts Fill

Which column a shift with Force Sunday Rate ticked fills. Check your payroll mapping before switching.

Forced Rates Only Apply to Worked Hours

Force OT / Force Sunday Rate leave paid leave on that day as Normal time, and never apply to an Open Shift.

Shift's OT Rule Decides Saturdays

A shift's "Override OT Rule" also decides whether Saturday is automatic overtime (otherwise only the employee's own rule or the company setting does).

Use the Shift's OT Auto Break

Takes the shift's "OT Auto Break" off the overtime once it reaches "Min OT to Trigger" (e.g. 30 min off when OT ≥ 2 h). Check which shifts have a value before switching on.

Own Default Shift: Unticked Days Are Days Off

Someone with their own default shift is off on the days not ticked (same as Auto-populate Roster). Default: those days fall back to the department's shift.

Employee Costs Use the Pay Rate Multipliers

Employee Costs uses the OT / Sunday / Holiday rates from Overtime Rules instead of the fixed 1.5× and 2.0×. Make sure payroll does not apply the rates a second time.

Label Threshold-Clamped Days As "Ignored"

A weekday whose early / late minutes all fell inside the thresholds gets the OT status Ignored instead of Pending. The Team View then shows the shift times instead of the real clock times. Hours do not change.
