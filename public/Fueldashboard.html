                Feature Module Settings
                Farming Department Tracking
                Adds an optional department field to manual entries and the phone fuel form.
                Show National Road on the Phone
                If off, the phone does not show 'National Road Used' (saved as No). You can still set it on Manual Entry and Edit.
                Typo Checks (Ask for a Reason)
                If on, litres more than the tank holds, an odometer lower than last time or a very big jump must be explained (typed reason) before saving. If off, nothing is asked, but the phone still refuses a lower odometer. Future dates are always refused.
                Asset Tagging Services Update the Service Schedule
                If on, a vehicle service logged in Asset Tagging (web or phone) sets the vehicle's Last Service KM here, so Next Service follows. The odometer and the service interval always stay in step between Fuel and Asset Tagging.
                Default National Road Policy
                If on, 'National Road Used' starts ticked for new fuel entries (web and phone).
                Mandatory Odometer Capture
                If on, a fill-up cannot be saved without an odometer / hour reading.
                Mandatory Phone Photos
                If on, the phone requires the odometer, before and after photos.
                KM Efficiency Display
                How vehicles tracked in KM are shown.
                HRS Efficiency Display
                How machines tracked in HRS are shown.
                Allowed Report Domains
                Scheduled reports can go to your own users, plus addresses on these domains (comma separated, e.g. accountants.co.za).
                Deliveries & Collections
                The delivery settings could not be loaded, so they are locked to protect them. Refresh the page to try again.
                Mandatory Evidence Photo
                The driver must photograph the goods at the stop before it can be saved.
                Mandatory POD Photo
                The signed proof of delivery document must be photographed.
                Mandatory Client Signature
                The person receiving the goods must sign on the phone.
                Allow Partial Deliveries
                Drivers may deliver less than planned. The stop becomes Partial and waits in Resolve.
                Allow Unplanned Stops
                Drivers may capture a delivery or collection that was never loaded on the system.
                Mandatory Driver Signature
                The driver signs the ePOD as well as the client.
                Damaged Goods Need A Photo
                Marking a line Damaged forces the driver to write a note and take a photo.
                Email The ePOD Automatically
                As soon as a stop syncs, the PDF is emailed to the client. Off means you send it by hand.
                ePOD For Failed Stops
                A PDF is also produced when a stop could not be completed.
                Photos On The ePOD
                Adds the POD, evidence, damage and extra photos on a page after the signatures.
                Office Copy For Opted-Out Clients
                The client gets nothing, but the CC address below still receives the ePOD.
